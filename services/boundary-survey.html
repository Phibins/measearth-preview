Boundary survey and property line staking

We find the legal corners of your property, replace any that are missing and mark the line on the ground so you and your neighbour can both see it.

When you need it

Before you build near the line

  • You are building a fence, retaining wall, driveway or hedge along the boundary
  • You and a neighbour disagree about where the line is
  • You cannot find your corner bars, or one has been disturbed
  • You are clearing or cutting trees near the edge of a rural or waterfront lot
  • A contractor needs the line marked before starting

What you receive

Corners and lines you can see

  • Research of the registered plans, deeds and earlier surveys that define your boundary
  • Each corner found, or a new survey monument set where one is missing
  • Stakes along the line at the spacing you ask for
  • A written report or sketch of what we found and set, where the job calls for one
  • A surveyor you can call if a neighbour or contractor has questions

Good to know

Why an app or a fence is not the line

Property-line apps and municipal maps draw boundaries from approximate data and can be out by a metre or more. Old fences were often built where it was convenient.

In Ontario only a licensed Ontario Land Surveyor can establish a boundary. We work from the registered documents and the physical evidence in the ground, which is what a court would look at.

If a lender, lawyer or municipality has asked for a survey, you probably need an SRPR as well. We can price both together.

Common questions

Questions we hear most

How much of the line do you mark?

As much as you need. Some clients want only the corners, others want a stake every few metres along a long or wooded line. Tell us what you are building and we will suggest a spacing.

What if my neighbour disagrees with the result?

We can explain what the boundary is based on. Our report sets out the evidence we relied on, and it is signed by an Ontario Land Surveyor.

Do you set permanent markers?

Where a corner monument is missing we set a new survey bar. Line stakes between corners are wooden and temporary, so build soon after we mark.
