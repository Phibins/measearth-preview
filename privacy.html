Privacy policy

What MeasEarth Inc. collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

MeasEarth Inc. is a land surveying firm at 244 Barrie St, Thornton, Ontario L0L 2N0. This policy covers the personal information we collect through measearth.com.

What we collect

  • Details you send through our quote and contact forms or chat: your name, phone number, email address, the property address and anything you tell us or attach about your project.
  • Calls to the phone number on this site. We use a call-tracking service that records which page or advertisement led to the call.
  • Usage data collected by cookies and similar tools, such as the pages you view and the site or advertisement that brought you here.

Why we collect it

  • To prepare the quote you asked for and to reply to you.
  • To carry out the survey if you hire us, and to keep the survey records our profession requires.
  • To understand which pages and advertisements are useful, so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

We use third-party services to run this site and handle enquiries: Coax for forms, chat and text messages, a call-tracking provider, and Google Analytics. These providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States and Australia.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become jobs are kept only as long as we need them to follow up. Survey job files, including client contact details, are kept as part of our permanent survey records.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent to being contacted. Call 705 434 0411 or write to the address above. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
